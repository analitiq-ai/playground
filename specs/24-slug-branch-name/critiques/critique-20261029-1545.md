## Executive Summary

The spec and plan are small, consistent and testable. The six clarification answers are reflected in the
requirements, the CLI contract pins every output stream and exit code, and the research justifies each mechanism
(ASCII-only translation table, `findall` + `join`, raw argv) against the specific edge cases it must meet. No
finding blocks tasks. Two recommendations tighten an untested boundary (an empty-string argument) and an
unverifiable success criterion; two questions concern where the tool lives and whether its tests run anywhere.
